The models

Flame-W (full40)
Predicts whole words (16,384-word vocabulary). Next-word top-1 accuracy 20.7% on chat and 18.5% on everyday text, 6.84–7.0 bits per word.
Ember (v0.2A)
Predicts one byte at a time, with no vocabulary. The model file is 4.5 MB. It scores 1.86–1.88 bits per byte on chat and everyday text.

These are early research models. Their sentences often don't make sense yet. Your ratings and corrections are what this site collects to improve them.
